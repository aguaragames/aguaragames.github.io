Privacy Policy

Español · English · Português

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

This policy explains what data Deck of Kings, the Android game by Aguará Games, uses, what it is used for, who it is shared with and how you can ask for it to be deleted. If you have any questions, write to us at contacto@aguaragames.com.

What we don't ask for

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or location, and there is no chat between players.

What data the game uses

  • Anonymous account. The first time you open the game, an automatic identifier is created to recognise your saved game. It is not linked to your name or email address.
  • Player name and code. The game gives you an automatic name (for example, "Jugador-4821") that you can change in Settings, and an 8-character player code. Other players see your name in online matches, and your name and code in their friends lists. Choose a name that doesn't reveal who you are.
  • Friends. If you add friends, we store your friends list and the requests you send and receive (their codes and names). To invite a friend, the game opens your phone's share menu; whatever you send there is handled by the app you choose, not by us.
  • Name reports. If you report another player's name, we store that name, their code and identifier, your identifier and the date, so we can review the case. If someone reports your name, the same is stored.
  • Game progress. Your cards, decks, gold, rubies, packs, cosmetics, league points, online match history, pass and event progress, and credited purchases.
  • Online matches. To connect you with another player, our server receives your IP address, name, code, cosmetics, deck and moves. It keeps technical logs (which include the IP address) for 30 days and played matches for 90 days, to solve problems, enforce the rules and keep the game secure.
  • Internet check. The game needs internet access. To find out whether there is a connection, it briefly contacts Google and Cloudflare servers. This check carries none of your data; those services only see your IP address, as with any connection.
  • Usage statistics (only if you agree). The first time you open the game, we ask whether you allow us to collect usage and device data: for example, matches started, finished and abandoned, their duration, the cards played and your phone model. If you don't agree, nothing is sent. You can change your answer at any time in Settings ("Statistics").
  • Error reports. If the game fails or crashes, a technical report is sent automatically (for example, the error, phone model, Android and game version, and session data) so we can fix it. These reports are sent even if you haven't agreed to statistics, because they are needed for the game to work properly.
  • Advertising. The game shows video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a reward. To show them, Google (AdMob) may use data from your device, such as the advertising ID and your IP address. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Purchases. Google Play processes payments. We do not receive or store your card details; only the receipt needed to confirm the purchase and deliver what you bought.

What we use it for

  • Saving your progress and making the game work.
  • Organising online matches, enforcing their rules and calculating leagues and rewards.
  • Showing your name to other players and managing your friends list.
  • Reviewing reported names and changing those that break the terms of use.
  • Confirming purchases and delivering what you bought.
  • Showing ads.
  • Fixing errors, improving the game and its balance (with statistics, only if you agreed) and keeping the game secure.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. The services that make the game work process it on our behalf or under their own policies:

  • Unity: anonymous account, saved game, cloud functions, names, friends, reports, usage statistics and error reports.
  • Google: ads (AdMob), purchases and distribution on Google Play, and the internet check.
  • Cloudflare: the internet check.
  • Oracle: hosts the online match server, located in São Paulo, Brazil.

Other players see your name, code, cosmetics and what you do in the online matches they play with you.

Because of these services, your data may be processed outside your country.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, progress, name and friends: as long as your account exists or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Server technical logs (with the IP address): 30 days.
  • Played online matches: 90 days.
  • Name reports: 1 year from the report.
  • Purchase receipts: as long as needed to prevent fraud and comply with the law, even if you delete your account.
  • Usage statistics and error reports: the period set by Unity.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account from the game: Settings > Delete my account. Your account, progress, name, code and friends list are deleted (you disappear from other players' lists), and we ask Unity to delete your usage statistics. If you no longer have the game installed, follow the steps on this page.

The following are kept for the periods listed above: server technical logs and saved matches, name reports and purchase receipts (marked as used, so they cannot be charged again).

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, and withdraw your consent to statistics at any time. To do so, write to us at contacto@aguaragames.com with your player code. You can also contact the data protection authority in your country.

Children

The game is for players aged 16 and over and is not directed at children under that age. If you believe a child under 16 has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

The game's connections to our servers are encrypted. No system is perfect, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with its date. If the change is important, we will also announce it in the game.
