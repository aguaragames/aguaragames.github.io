Delete your Deck of Kings account

Español · English · Português

Last updated: 14 November 2026.

This page explains how to ask for your Deck of Kings account, the Android game by Aguará Games, and your data to be deleted.

If you have the game installed

Open the game and tap Settings > Delete my account. After you confirm twice, the account is deleted straight away.

If you no longer have the game

  • Write to us at contacto@aguaragames.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  • Include your player code: 8 letters and numbers, such as "ABCD-EFGH". In the game it appears in Settings, below the title, and in your profile. If you no longer have it, your in-game friends can see it in their friends list. If there is no way to get it, tell us your player name and when you last played, and we will help you find your account.
  • To check that the account is yours, we may ask you something only its user knows (for example, your league or your decks).
  • We delete your account within 30 days at most and let you know by email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous account.
  • Your progress: cards, decks, gold, rubies, packs, skins, league points, match history, and season pass and event progress.
  • Your name, your code and your friends list (you disappear from other players' lists).
  • Your usage statistics: we ask Unity to delete them.

Anything you bought with real money (rubies and "No ads") is also lost.

What is kept

  • Technical server logs (with the IP address): 30 days.
  • Online matches played: 90 days.
  • Name reports: 1 year from the report.
  • Purchase receipts: marked as used and not linked to your account, so they cannot be used again; for as long as needed to prevent fraud and comply with the law.
  • Crash reports: for the time set by Unity.
